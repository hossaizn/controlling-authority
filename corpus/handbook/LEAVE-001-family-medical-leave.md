# Family and Medical Leave

Meridian Freight Systems provides unpaid, job-protected family and medical leave to eligible employees in accordance with the federal Family and Medical Leave Act.

## Eligibility

You are eligible if you have worked for Meridian for at least 12 months, have worked at least 1,250 hours during the 12 months before your leave begins, and work at a location where the company employs 50 or more employees within 75 miles.

## Amount of leave

Eligible employees may take up to 12 workweeks of leave in a rolling 12-month period, measured backward from the date leave is used.

## Qualifying reasons

- The birth of a child, or placement of a child with you for adoption or foster care
- To care for your spouse, child, or parent with a serious health condition
- Your own serious health condition that makes you unable to perform your job
- Qualifying exigencies arising from a family member's covered military service

## Requesting leave

Where the need is foreseeable, give your manager and the People team at least 30 days notice. Where it is not, notify us as soon as practicable. We may require medical certification.

## Benefits during leave

Group health coverage continues on the same terms as if you were working. You remain responsible for your share of premiums.

## Return to work

On return from approved leave you will be restored to your original position or an equivalent one with equivalent pay, benefits and terms of employment.

## Interaction with state law

Where state law provides greater rights than this policy, state law applies. Speak to the People team about your specific situation.
