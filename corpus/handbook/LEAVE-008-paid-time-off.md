# Paid Time Off

Paid time off covers vacation and personal days. Sick leave is handled separately under the Paid Sick Leave policy.

## Accrual

| Years of service | Annual accrual |
|------------------|----------------|
| Under 2 years | 15 days |
| 2 to 5 years | 20 days |
| Over 5 years | 25 days |

Part-time employees accrue on a pro-rata basis.

## Requesting time off

Submit requests through the scheduling system at least two weeks in advance where possible. Approval depends on operational coverage, and during peak freight periods some requests may not be accommodated.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused days carry into the following year and must be used by 31 March. Days beyond that are forfeited.

## On leaving

Accrued and unused paid time off is handled according to the law of the state in which you work. The People team will confirm the position on your final pay.
