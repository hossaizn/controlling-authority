# Parental Leave

Meridian offers parental leave to help new parents bond with a child following birth, adoption or foster placement.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed **18 months of continuous service** at the time leave begins are eligible for parental leave.

## Amount of leave

Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of parental leave within the first 12 months following the child's birth or placement. Parental leave runs concurrently with any leave taken under the Family and Medical Leave policy for the same event.

## Pay

The first 4 weeks are paid at 100% of base salary. The remaining 8 weeks are unpaid, though you may elect to use accrued paid time off.

## Both parents

Where both parents work for Meridian, each is separately eligible.

## Requesting leave

Notify the People team at least 30 days before your intended start date where possible, along with expected dates of leave and return.
