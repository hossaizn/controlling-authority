# Bereavement Leave

We recognise that time away is necessary after the death of someone close to you.

## Amount of leave

All employees may take up to **10 days of paid bereavement leave** per occurrence following the death of a family member. There is no minimum service requirement.

## Who counts as a family member

Spouse or domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, parent-in-law, and any individual whose close association with you was the equivalent of a family relationship.

## Taking the leave

Days need not be taken consecutively but should be taken within three months of the death. Longer arrangements can be discussed with your manager.

## Documentation

We do not ordinarily require documentation. In unusual circumstances the People team may ask for reasonable verification.
