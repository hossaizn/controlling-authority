# Military Leave

Meridian supports employees serving in the uniformed services and complies with the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act.

## Leave for service

Employees called to active duty, active duty for training, initial active duty for training, inactive duty training, or a fitness-for-duty examination are granted unpaid leave for the period of service.

## Notice

Give your manager and the People team as much advance notice as is reasonable, in writing where possible, unless military necessity prevents it.

## Benefits during leave

Health coverage may be continued for up to 24 months during military leave. Where your service is 30 days or fewer, coverage continues on the same terms as active employment.

## Reemployment

On timely return you will be reemployed in the position you would have held had you remained continuously employed, or a position of like seniority, status and pay, provided the conditions of USERRA are met.

## Military family leave

Time off for qualifying exigencies arising from a family member's covered active duty is handled under the Family and Medical Leave policy.
