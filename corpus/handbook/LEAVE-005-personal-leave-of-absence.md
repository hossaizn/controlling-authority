# Personal Leave of Absence

In limited circumstances, Meridian may grant an unpaid personal leave of absence for reasons not covered by our other leave policies.

## Who may request

Personal leave is available at management discretion to **established employees in good standing**. Requests from employees in their initial period, or from those working reduced schedules, are considered on a case-by-case basis.

## Amount

Personal leave is normally granted for up to 30 days. Extensions beyond this are unusual and require approval from a department head.

## Effect on benefits

Group health coverage may continue during personal leave, subject to the terms of the plan and your continued payment of premiums. Paid time off does not accrue during unpaid personal leave.

## Return to work

Reinstatement to your prior position is not guaranteed. We will make reasonable efforts to return you to a comparable role where one is available.

## How to request

Submit a written request to your manager and the People team describing the reason and the expected duration, ordinarily at least 30 days in advance.
