# Jury Duty, Witness Duty and Voting Leave

## Jury duty

Employees summoned for jury service are granted time off for the duration of the service. Meridian pays full base salary for the first 10 working days of jury service. Beyond that, leave is unpaid, though you may use accrued paid time off.

Provide a copy of your summons to your manager as soon as you receive it, and return to work on any day or partial day you are released from service.

## Witness duty

Employees subpoenaed as a witness are granted unpaid time off for the duration. Where you are appearing on behalf of Meridian, the time is paid.

## Voting leave

Employees who do not have sufficient time outside working hours to vote in a public election may take paid time off to do so. Arrange this with your manager at least two working days in advance so shift coverage can be arranged.

The amount of paid voting leave and the conditions attached vary by state. Contact the People team if you are unsure what applies at your location.
