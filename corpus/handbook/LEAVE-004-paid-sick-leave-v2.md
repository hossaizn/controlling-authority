# Paid Sick Leave

Employees accrue paid sick leave for their own illness or to care for a family member.

## Accrual

Employees accrue one hour of paid sick leave for every 30 hours worked, up to a maximum of **40 hours, or 5 days, per year**.

## Carryover

Unused sick leave carries over to the following year, though total accrual is capped at 80 hours at any time.

## Using sick leave

Sick leave may be used for your own diagnosis, care or treatment of an existing health condition, for preventive care, or to care for a family member for the same reasons. It may also be used if you are a victim of domestic violence, sexual assault or stalking.

## Notice

Where the need is foreseeable, give reasonable advance notice. Where it is not, notify your manager as soon as practicable.

## Rate of pay

Sick leave is paid at your regular rate of pay.
